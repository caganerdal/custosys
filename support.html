Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't Custosys show the temperature in degrees?
iOS and macOS only tell apps four heat levels (normal, warm, hot, critical). Reading exact temperatures requires private methods that the App Store doesn't allow.

Why is the charging power on iPhone “estimated”?
iOS doesn't share charging power with apps, so Custosys estimates it from how fast the battery level changes and your phone's battery capacity.

My iPhone can't find my Mac.
Open Custosys on your Mac and turn on monitoring under Sharing. Make sure both devices are on the same network and that Custosys is allowed in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. Some school and office networks block devices from seeing each other; use “Connect by address” or a home network.

How do I restore Custosys Pro on another device?
Pro works on all your devices signed in with the same Apple Account. If it isn't active, open the Pro screen and tap “Restore Purchases”.

Is my data sent anywhere?
No. See the privacy policy.

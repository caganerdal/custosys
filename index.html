Custosys is Latin for “keeper of the system”. It shows what your iPhone, iPad and Mac are really doing: CPU, memory, storage, network, battery and heat, all on your device.

Features

  • Live CPU, GPU, memory, storage and network monitoring
  • Speed test with responsiveness (RPM)
  • Performance test with a Custosys score
  • Mac power flow and battery health tracking, estimated charging power on iPhone
  • Monitor your Mac from your iPhone over an encrypted local connection
  • Wi‑Fi analyzer on Mac, hardware test on iPhone
  • Widgets, Mac menu bar and alerts
